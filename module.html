Every piece is separate: the base, each module and each roof. Modules stack on 3 mm porcelain lips (up to 8 high) and sit side by side wall to wall. For now everything is flush with the walls: no mouldings, balconies or overhangs. The slanted roof follows the manual's ~30° (p. 80); the modern roof is a thin flat slab. Both have a vent for the candle's heat.

Levels: plate and candle

Each level can sit on its own porcelain plate (the base at ground level) and carry its own candles, one tealight per module. A candle needs a plate under it. The roof covers the whole building in one piece (like the manual's red-tiled hip) or sits as a small lid on every top module. Plates and roofs have Ø 8 mm vents so the heat rises out.
